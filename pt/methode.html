Como funcionam os rankings?

RebornRank is a themed discovery site. It does not replace source databases: data is requested on demand and cached locally to limit calls.

Displayed covers come from cover URLs returned by the data source.
Rankings combine score, popularity and tag relevance.
Hidden Gems favors strong scores with lower popularity.
Adult content is excluded by default.
Regression and second-life categories combine related tags so fewer works are missed.
